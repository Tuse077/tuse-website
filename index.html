How it works

Booked to paid, in five steps.

No haggling at the gate. No chasing anyone on the phone. Your money sits in escrow until the work is done.

01Open the app

Download Tuse and create your account in under a minute.

02Book an artisan

Say what you need, see the price, and pick a verified professional near you.

03Pay into escrow

You pay up front, but the artisan does not receive it yet. Tuse holds the money.

04The artisan comes and does the work

Tracked to your door. The money is still held, so nothing is at risk until the job is finished.

05The money is released

Once the job is done, the payment goes to the artisan. That is the only point at which it leaves escrow.
