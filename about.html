We will fix it. That is the name.

Tușe, often typed Tuse, is a Nigerian platform for booking verified, skilled artisans. The name is the promise.

What does Tușe mean?

Tușe comes from the Yorùbá word túṣe, to fix or mend. Our line A máa túṣe means “We will fix it”, and we say it in every language our customers use:

  • Yorùbá: A máa túṣe.
  • Igbo: Anyị ga edozi ya.
  • Hausa: Za mu gyara shi.
  • Pidgin: We go fix am.
  • English: We will fix it.

The name is short on purpose. A leaking tap, a generator that will not start, braids at home on a Saturday, a deep clean before you move in: the answer is the same.

How do you say it?

Say it “TOO-sheh”. The Ș in the wordmark is a “sh” sound, the same as the Yorùbá ṣ. Most people type it without the accent, as Tuse, and that is fine. TUȘE, Tușe and Tuse are the same company and the same app.

What Tușe does

Tușe connects you with skilled artisans who have been checked before anyone can book them. That covers 19 services, from plumbing, electrical work, AC repair and generator repair to cleaning, barbing, hairdressing and makeup.

You describe the job in your own words in the app. iRanti, our assistant, works out what it likely involves and the expected price. A verified artisan near you is matched, you can track them to your door, and your payment is held in the app until the job is done.

Why we built it

Good hands shouldn't depend on who you know. Finding a reliable artisan in Nigeria has usually meant referral chains, unanswered calls, no-shows and cash disputes. We rebuilt the experience around four fixes:

  • Vetted before they're listed. Identity confirmed, skills assessed, address on file. If they're on Tușe, someone checked.
  • Matched by distance, not luck. The app finds who is genuinely closest to you and shows their arrival in real time.
  • Priced up front, paid in-app. You see the cost before you book, and payment is held until the job is done.
  • Rated after every single job. Reviews come only from customers who actually paid, so poor work follows an artisan.

Who it is for

  • Anyone who needs something fixed, cleaned, styled or cooked. Browse all services or start with a problem guide.
  • Artisans who want steady work and on-time pay. The Tuse Artisan app is how you join. See the two apps.
  • Businesses and property managers. One account for every property. Talk to us.
