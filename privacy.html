Privacy policy

How we collect, use, share and protect the information of users booking services, and of artisans and runners providing them.

Effective date: 2 September 2025

At Tușe, your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect the information of Users (those booking services) and Artisans & Runners (those providing services).

By using Tușe, you agree to this Policy.

1. Information we collect

For users

We may collect:

  • Personal information: name, phone number, email, profile photo.
  • Location data: to connect you with nearby artisans and errand runners.
  • Service information: bookings, messages, reviews, and ratings.
  • Payment information: billing details and transactions, processed by third-party payment providers.
  • Device and usage data: device type, operating system, and app activity.

For artisans and runners

We may collect:

  • Personal information: full name, phone number, email, profile photo.
  • Verification data: government ID, proof of skills and certifications, and background checks.
  • Financial information: bank account details for payouts.
  • Service data: jobs accepted, ratings, reviews, and performance records.
  • Location data: real-time location to match with nearby users.

2. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and improve our services.
  • Connect users with the right artisans and errand runners.
  • Verify artisan identities and qualifications.
  • Process bookings, payments, and payouts securely.
  • Offer customer support and handle disputes.
  • Send important updates, notifications, and promotional messages, with your consent.
  • Improve security and prevent fraud.

3. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal data. We may share information only in these cases:

  • Between users and artisans: limited details needed to complete bookings, such as first name, profile, and ratings.
  • With service providers: payment processors, hosting providers, and analytics tools.
  • For legal and safety reasons: to comply with laws, enforce our Terms, or protect Tușe, users, and artisans.

4. Data security

  • Verification and financial data are stored securely with restricted access.
  • Payments and payouts are encrypted and processed via trusted third-party providers.
  • We use safeguards to protect data, but no system is 100% secure.

5. Your rights

Both users and artisans may:

  • Access and update their personal information.
  • Request deletion of their account and related data.
  • Withdraw consent for promotional communications.

For requests, email support@tuse.ng.

6. Children's privacy

Tușe is not directed at anyone under 18. If we discover we have collected information from a minor, we will delete it.

7. Updates to this policy

We may update this Policy periodically. Changes will be reflected by the effective date and communicated in-app or by email.

8. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at:
