AC Not Turning On? Safe Checks Before You Call

AC repair on Tuse starts from ₦12,000. Describe the problem in the app — iRanti helps with a preliminary category and price band before anyone arrives.

The short answer

No power often comes from a dead remote battery, a tripped breaker, a loose plug, or an internal protection trip. Stay on the safe side of the switchboard — do not open the outdoor electrical panel yourself.

Common causes

  • Remote batteries flat or remote IR not reaching the unit
  • Wall isolator or plug switched off
  • Breaker tripped on the consumer unit
  • Failed capacitor, control board, or protection lockout after a fault

What you can safely check

These are low-risk checks only. Do not open live electrical panels, handle refrigerant, or dismantle fuel systems. If anything feels unsafe, stop and book a verified professional.

  1. Try new remote batteries, or use the button on the indoor unit if it has one
  2. Confirm the wall switch or plug for that AC is on
  3. Check whether the breaker labelled for that circuit has tripped — reset once only if you know it is the correct breaker
  4. If power is present but the unit stays silent, note any error code on the display for the technician

When to call a professional

  • Burning smell or scorched plug
  • Breaker trips again immediately after reset
  • Visible damage to wiring or the outdoor isolator

FAQs

Why won't my AC turn on?

Power supply, remote batteries, a tripped breaker, or an internal fault are common. Repeated trips need an electrician or AC technician — do not keep resetting.
