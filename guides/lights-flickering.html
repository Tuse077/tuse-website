Lights Flickering? When It Is Urgent

Electrical work on Tuse starts from ₦10,000. Describe the problem in the app — iRanti helps with a preliminary category and price band before anyone arrives.

The short answer

Occasional flicker when a heavy appliance starts can be normal on a weak supply. Constant flicker, flicker in one room, or flicker with buzzing suggests a loose connection that can overheat — treat that as urgent.

Common causes

  • Loose bulb in the holder
  • Failing LED driver or incompatible dimmer
  • Loose neutral or connection in a fitting or junction
  • Supply voltage fluctuation from the network or generator AVR

What you can safely check

These are low-risk checks only. Do not open live electrical panels, handle refrigerant, or dismantle fuel systems. If anything feels unsafe, stop and book a verified professional.

  1. Tighten the bulb with the light off and cool
  2. Note whether flicker is whole-house or one fitting
  3. See if flicker coincides with fridge, AC, or pump start-up
  4. Do not open ceiling roses or consumer units

When to call a professional

  • Burning smell from fittings
  • Visible arcing or sparking
  • Flicker plus warm switches or faceplates

How Tușe and iRanti help

Open the Tuse app and describe what is happening in your own words — for example, “lights flickering”. iRanti reads the description, suggests the likely service category and a price band, and routes you toward a verified electrical work professional. It is a preliminary assessment, not a definitive remote diagnosis: the artisan still inspects on site before any repair.

Why do my lights flicker?

Loose bulbs, LED issues, supply dips, or unsafe loose wiring. Persistent flicker deserves an electrician.
