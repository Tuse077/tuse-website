Leaking Tap? Common Causes and Fixes

Plumbing on Tuse starts from ₦10,000. Describe the problem in the app — iRanti helps with a preliminary category and price band before anyone arrives.

The short answer

A dripping tap is often a worn washer, cartridge, or O-ring. It is usually straightforward for a plumber; forced DIY on old fittings can crack the body and flood the cabinet.

Common causes

  • Worn washer or ceramic cartridge
  • Loose packing nut on older pillar taps
  • Damaged O-ring on the spout
  • High water pressure accelerating wear

What you can safely check

These are low-risk checks only. Do not open live electrical panels, handle refrigerant, or dismantle fuel systems. If anything feels unsafe, stop and book a verified professional.

  1. Note whether the drip is from the spout, the handle base, or under the sink
  2. Check under the sink for a wet supply hose connection
  3. Close the under-sink isolation valves if present before any work
  4. Avoid overtightening the handle — that can worsen washer damage

When to call a professional

  • Water spraying from a cracked tap body
  • Cabinet floor soaking with no clear source
  • Cannot isolate the tap and drip is heavy

How Tușe and iRanti help
