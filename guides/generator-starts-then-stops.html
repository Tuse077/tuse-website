Generator Starts Then Stops? What It Often Means

Generator repair on Tuse starts from ₦12,000. Describe the problem in the app — iRanti helps with a preliminary category and price band before anyone arrives.

The short answer

Running briefly then dying often points to fuel delivery, a dirty carburettor jet, low oil protection, overload, or overheating. Stop restarting in a loop — note the pattern for the technician.

Common causes

  • Partly blocked fuel filter or stale fuel
  • Low oil sensor cutting the engine
  • Overload from too many appliances
  • Overheating from poor ventilation or low oil
  • Carburettor gumming after long storage

What you can safely check

These are low-risk checks only. Do not open live electrical panels, handle refrigerant, or dismantle fuel systems. If anything feels unsafe, stop and book a verified professional.

  1. Reduce load: disconnect non-essential appliances and try again once
  2. Verify oil level and fuel quality (old fuel smells varnish-like)
  3. Ensure the set has open air around it while running outdoors
  4. Do not keep cranking if it dies the same way each time

When to call a professional

  • Overheating smell or hot metal you can feel from a safe distance
  • Hunting RPM then abrupt shutdown with black smoke
  • Oil warning light or alarm if fitted
