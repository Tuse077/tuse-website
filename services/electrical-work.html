Verified electrical work, booked through Tuse.

Sockets, wiring, faults, fittings. From ₦10,000, held in the app and released only once the job is done.

What this covers

Tuse's electrical work category covers sockets, wiring, faults, fittings. People typically book it for:

  • a socket or switch that has stopped working
  • wiring or fitting faults
  • new sockets, switches or light fittings installed

Pricing

Electrical work on Tuse starts from ₦10,000. That is a starting price, not a guaranteed final one — the actual cost depends on the scope of the job, any materials or parts required, and how complex the work turns out to be once the artisan looks at it. You see the price before you confirm the booking, and if it needs to change, the artisan raises a revised price in the app that you approve or decline before any extra work begins.

Not sure exactly what you need?

Describe it in your own words in the Tuse app — whether something has broken or you just want to book electrical work for a specific day. iRanti reads what you type, works out what the job most likely involves and the likely price band, and routes you to the right verified professional.

FAQs

How much does electrical work cost on Tuse?

Prices vary by category, so the exact cost is confirmed in the app before you book. The final price can depend on the scope of the job, materials or parts needed, and how complex the work is. If the job turns out to be bigger once the artisan is there, they raise a revised price in the app with a reason, and you approve or decline it before any extra work begins.

Are Tuse's electrical work professionals verified?

Yes. Before an artisan can be booked, they clear identity verification (government ID confirmed, face matched to the ID), a trade competence assessment for the categories they list, a home address on record, and a bank account tied to their own verified identity.

When do I pay?

You pay into the app when you book, but the money is held in escrow rather than sent to the artisan straight away. It is released once the job is marked complete, so you are not paying before the work is done.

Where does Tuse offer electrical work?

Coverage depends on which verified artisans are active near you. Abuja, Lagos and Port Harcourt currently have the deepest coverage. Open the Tuse app and enter your address to see who is available there right now.

How do I book?

Download the Tuse app, describe what you need — in your own words, iRanti works out the category — and you will be matched with a verified professional near you. You can track them to your door and pay in the app once the job is done.
